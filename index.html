LeakGuard: catching revenue leakage in campus shops

Batch B, Group 41  ·  Indian Institute of Technology Madras  ·  15 November 2026

Abstract. Campus canteens, stationery shops and general stores lose money in three ways: shoplifting, stock that goes missing between deliveries and audits, and refunds that were never earned. Most small owners have no affordable way to see any of the three. LeakGuard ties all three to one source of truth, the bill. Every sale gets a bill ID, every stock count is checked against billed sales, every refund must match a real bill, and a browser-based camera model flags people who leave without one. This working prototype runs on simulated shop data; the campus figures and benchmarks are real and cited.
